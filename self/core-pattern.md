# Core Pattern & Minimum Viable Vision

(Source: Psychological Excavation, June 2026)

Subject to change — living document.

---

## The Core Pattern

Not a skill gap. A completion + visibility gap.

- I start things and don't finish them.
- I build in isolation rather than putting work out before it feels complete.
- The fear isn't failure — it's being seen at an unfinished stage and judged against where I "should" be by now.
- Self-protection mechanism: avoid exposure of past gaps by not shipping/posting. Cost: the exact proof of work that would close those gaps never gets created.

---

## Identity Shift Required

**From:** "Someone who starts things and doesn't finish them. Who gets ideas and leaves them hanging. Who builds without putting work out there."

**To:** "Someone with verifiable expertise who is dogged once committed — who seizes an idea and sees it through to a working result."

---

## Minimum Viable Vision (Q1 2026 — the actual target)

Age ~31, married, financially stable through own businesses, MSc completed, working in advanced AI on products with real reach, living where I choose, able to support and reach family without strain.

An average Tuesday: workout, check on businesses, build work with real impact, home with family before 10pm.

The single near-term action this implies: "Churn out God-inspired level work and write about it." Not new work. Existing work, finished and made visible.

---

## Decision Filter — Add This Question

Before starting anything new, ask: **"Do I have an unfinished/unpublished thing that this new idea is distracting me from?"** If yes — finish or ship that first.

---

## Operating Rule

Track **CLOSED LOOPS**, not started ones. A closed loop = something taken from "done in private" to "visible/shipped," however small (a write-up, a deployed feature, a submitted application, a finished module).

If closed-loop count is zero for 2+ days — Kaizen it: shrink the task until finishing it today is unavoidable.

---

## Anti-Vision Reminder (use sparingly, only when stuck)

The cost of not breaking this pattern isn't neutral — it's the Brother Monday / Adex trajectory: real talent, no completed proof of it, eventually forced into whatever pays. The five/ten-year version isn't a vague fear, it's a specific, named outcome to actively design away from.
