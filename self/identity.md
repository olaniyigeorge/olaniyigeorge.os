## Personal Mission Statement

I am Abeleje Olaniyi George, a software engineer and builder.

I am a child of God, and I live to reflect His nature in how I think, build, and serve.

I value humility, continuous learning, and self-awareness — the discipline to recognize when I am wrong and grow from it.

Through the work of my hands and my presence, I aim to be an outreach — creating things that solve real problems and continue to impact lives long after I am gone.

I do this because I believe my life must serve something greater than myself.

My purpose is to glorify God and make it easier for others to live meaningful, aligned lives.
