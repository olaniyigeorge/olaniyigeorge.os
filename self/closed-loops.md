## How to Use

Every time something gets finished and made visible — a write-up, a deployed feature, a submitted application, a finished module, a CV tailored and sent, an article posted — log it here with the date.

This is the infrastructure for the operating rule from `core-pattern.md`: "Track CLOSED LOOPS, not started ones."
